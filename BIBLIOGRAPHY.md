# Essential annotated bibliography and novelty assessment

Search date: 24 October 2026. Targeted primary-source search, not a completed systematic review. Searches covered Ranaudo/HADDOCK, glycan/GAG docking benchmarks, AAV9 galactose, AAV2 heparin, current software documentation and 2025–2026 follow-up work. No claim of exhaustive novelty clearance. Source verification levels are explicit below.

## Defensible contribution

A generic program comparison, carbohydrate torsion analysis, GAG scoring critique or water-guided improvement alone is insufficiently novel. The proposed contribution is a family-aware, assembly-aware comparison of **information regimes**, connected to a failure audit: reference quality → site coverage → returned candidate quality → ranking → experimental specificity. AAV9/AAV2 are contrasting case studies, not the independent statistical population.

The most valuable ablations are complete neighborhood versus isolated subunit, true-site versus predicted-site search, dry versus justified retained-water treatment, and raw versus size/charge-matched specificity. Novelty remains provisional until the 2026 paper's supplement and earlier benchmark overlaps are checked. No additional simulations should precede that overlap audit and the reference-quality gate.
